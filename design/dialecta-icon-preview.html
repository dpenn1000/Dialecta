Option 1 · Asterism

22px metallic strip with a centered · ⁂ ·. Decorative only, no per-page logic, lowest implementation cost. Acknowledges the double-bar identity without earning its space functionally.

Option 2 · Breadcrumb

28px metallic strip with section + page name in DM Mono uppercase. Earns the space: orients the reader to where they are. Article shown above; on home it's just DIALECTA, on profile it's PROFILE · NAME.
