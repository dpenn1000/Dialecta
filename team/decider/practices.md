# Standing practices

A practice is settled until evidence moves it. Confidence is the agent's own read.
Evidence names the file in `knowledge/` that backs it, or `(unsourced)` when nothing does.

| Practice | Confidence | Evidence | Last changed |

| One decision at a time, stated in a sentence, with what it blocks in the backlog named | high | `.claude/agents/decider.md` | 2026-09-19 |
| If a spec has already decided it, say so and stop; the job is then a builder brief | high | `.claude/agents/decider.md` | 2026-09-19 |
| Two to four options, each argued as its proponent would argue it, then one recommendation with the uncertainty named plainly | high | `.claude/agents/decider.md` | 2026-09-19 |

| Writes only into `docs/decisions/` and `council/log/`, and never edits a spec | high | `.claude/agents/decider.md`; `.claude/hooks/guard-docs.mjs` | 2026-09-19 |
| Record the condition the decision rests on, not only the reason that carried it. | When an option list arrives from someone else, check the options are parallel before arguing them. | When Dan overrules the chair, quote him in the record and say the chair was overruled. A reversal that reads as if it were always the plan teaches the next reader nothing | high | `council/log/2026-09-19-advisor-mandates.md`; `exchange/README.md` learning loop | 2026-09-19 |
| A record closes when the question it asks is answered, not when the work it points at is finished. Evidence that resolves the posed question is enough to close; unfinished downstream work (a rewrite, a fix, a migration) stays tracked in its own runbook or backlog row, named in the outcome line rather than left to keep the record open | high | `exchange/closed/2026-09-19-001-advice-supabase-schema-collision.md`, closed on migrator's forensic evidence rather than on Dan's own words | 2026-09-20 |
| An advice record asking this seat to route, escalate now versus hold, rather than to rule on the substance, is decidable without Dan. It is a chair judgment call, not a locked-decision override; answer it, close it, and carry the substance to Dan directly rather than leaving the record open as a proxy for "Dan hasn't seen this yet" | high | `exchange/closed/2026-09-19-004-advice-tier-palette-contrast-defects.md` | 2026-09-20 |
| Why the close step went unused for 21+ records across two days: filing has a forcing function, the record is the visible unit of work, and closing does not. Blindspots and votes are designed not to block by rule, so nothing requires anyone to return to them. A handoff closes only when someone remembers to edit the file after picking up the work, and picking up the work is what gets tracked, not the edit. No seat's mandate but the chair's names sweeping the exchange itself as a job, and even the chair's own prior brief listed closing one specific vote, not a standing sweep. The fix is a recurring close pass, not better manners from nine agents each focused on their own next record | high | this sprint: 25 records read, 3 closable on evidence, 0 previously closed ever | 2026-09-20 |
| Superseded is not the only state for a decision a later one narrows. "Amends," the earlier record stays Accepted and is cross-referenced by the later one rather than marked Superseded, is a real pattern in practice, not a deviation to correct. Offer it to Dan as an option next to Superseded when a later decision narrows rather than reverses an earlier one | medium | `knowledge/2018-npryce-adrtools-amendment-case.md` | 2026-09-20 |
| Weigh a proposed new ADR field against the lean-format evidence, not only the more-fields evidence, before it reaches Dan as if the case were one-sided. The Y-statement format drops Status and Assumptions on purpose and argues a shorter record travels further; `## Holds while` argues the opposite. Both can be right for different failure modes, but the tension is Dan's to see, not this seat's to resolve quietly | medium | `knowledge/2013-zdun-sustainable-architectural-decisions.md` | 2026-09-20 |
| Other seats write to `exchange/` and `council/log/` concurrently with this one.
