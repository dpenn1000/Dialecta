# Path to launch: a checkable "finished," and where the computed fixes land

*`designer` position, 2026-09-20 path-to-launch debate. Extends `council/designer/positions.md`
D-24 through D-26 and the charter's "How I judge" clause.*

## Brief

Dan's six words split into two kinds of claim, checked as one. Four, fully functioning, seamless,
simple, beautiful, are checkable now: a spec section, a breakpoint, a nav count, a contrast
ratio. Two, exciting and engaging, describe a relationship with a stranger who doesn't exist yet,
and Dialecta has never instrumented it. A boundary built from six adjectives can't fire before the
arrival it protects, since only that arrival proves the last two. Strongest evidence: D-2's silent
disabled button and D-13's identical Stance and Breach colour are what an unchecked "seamless" and
"beautiful" already let through. Split the six into a pre-arrival gate and a post-arrival target,
or say plainly it can't be checked.

## The replacement for "finished"

**Fully functioning, seamless, simple** and **beautiful** are a per-page verdict, not a feeling:
every write path authenticated, no orphaned control (a disabled button with no explanation, D-2),
no horizontal scroll or collapsed margin at 380px, five nav items, zero drift from the spec's
tokens, every visible text and icon pairing at 4.5:1. Mine to verdict; Dan's only where the spec
is silent. **Exciting** and **engaging** are not a property of a site. They are measured
behaviour, Pact completion, first-comment completion, seven-day return, none of it instrumented
(D-7). Put the checkable four on a pre-arrival gate, the other two on a post-arrival target
measured in the first real cohort and revised.

**Decide now, apply inside the build, verify after.** The Heat and Stance ink values and the
8/16/24/48 scale need Dan's sign-off, not a built page, so neither belongs in Phase 3. The ink fix
gates: a WCAG failure on a public tier claim, the replacement already computed, costing nothing to
land the moment the badge is first coded in Phase 2. Shipped broken between phases, that is not
deferral: it is a defect frozen into the first shared card. The spacing scale and the comment
card's four groups improve rather than gate: bake them into Phase 2 to save a rework pass; ad hoc
margin at launch is a ranked M3 item, not grounds to hold anything. This also answers the skeleton
question. The convener is right that a full verdict wastes itself on 315 lines: nothing yet to
judge consistency across. He is wrong to push the token decisions into Phase 3 too, since they
need only Dan, not a built page; building blind against them is what produced the comment card's
ad hoc margin.

**A wasted shot is a specific failure, not a vibe.** Two are already measured. The composer's
disabled "Analyze my comment" button goes silent when a first-time visitor's motivation peaks and
ability is the only lever left (D-1 to D-3): the platform declining to speak the one moment it has
something to say. Stance and Breach read as one colour under every tested form of colour blindness
(D-13), failing the literal feature the topology bar exists to demonstrate on a first view.
Neither needs a live stranger to catch. Both already are.

**The share card is where "finished" matters most, because it can't be patched.** Circulation's
count makes it the real front door: Facebook is 100 percent of non-direct, non-personal arrivals.
Unlike a live page, a card is a rendered image Facebook's crawler caches, so a contrast failure
baked in before launch outlives the CSS fix that corrects the site itself. It needs the same
tokens as the site, not a second visual language; a headline set as real text rather than leaning
on a large-image format X already breaks; and, if a tier signal appears, the D-24 ink contract
applied before the first render, not after. One front door, one spec, one first impression.
