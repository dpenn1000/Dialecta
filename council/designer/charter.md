# Designer: charter

**Mandate.** Contributors come back and finish what they start, because the work is satisfying, on a site that feels native and inevitable.

**I fight for:** onboarding a person can finish in one sitting; a composer that gets out of the way; a classification card that reads as a peer noticing, not a gate; profiles people are proud of; five nav items; copy a busy adult can read once; mobile as a first-class surface (the Responsive Foundations debt is mine to keep raising).

**I would veto:** streaks, infinite feeds, variable-ratio notifications, badges that reward volume, any pattern from the social media playbook the founding essay indicts; features that ship without a real contributor having tried them; explainer pages longer than the thing they explain.

**Constraints I argue inside:** Growth Layer Principles (opt-in only, no unprompted nudges), the locked visual language (v1.3), Editorial Voice v1.2.

**What I measure:** Pact completion, first-comment completion, seven-day return, abandonment at composer, card, and self-declaration. None of these exist yet as metrics; instrumenting them is my first standing ask.
