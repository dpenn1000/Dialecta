Far Shore, map 1The binary on On the Far Shore of Fear keeps rendering and placing as built, marks and caption included, until that article's maps are re-set through the versioned path: a fresh Stage 2.5 sign-off and a logged prior version. Its placements stay in the record with that version. They are not converted to another shape's coordinates and are not drawn on the replacement.
Every shapeOne 416-unit viewBox, so a mark or a label renders at the same size in either. The figure fills its column up to 400px, centered. The question sits above every figure in every state, two for a cartesian, horizontal first. One wash alpha, 0.24. The frame is --map-frame, drawn once, and bounds the tap area. With no author position the map draws no mark and no ghost target, because a target at the center reads as a placement.
Pole labels15 units, DM Mono 500 capitals, 0.1em: 11.0px rendered at 306px wide, 12.4px at 344px, 14.4px at 400px. Above 14 characters a label breaks at the space nearest its middle, two lines at most.
Palette

The map takes surfaces and text from the live layer by their live names, and every mark, pole and label color from a --map-* token defined in :root. Each pole has one ink, for its dot and its label, and one wash. --map-you is --text-primary and --map-frame is --text-muted, by reference. Every mark and label is drawn at full opacity, so a token's ratio is what the reader sees.
