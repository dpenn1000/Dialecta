# A century of Gestalt psychology in visual perception: I. Perceptual grouping and figure-ground organization

**Source:** Johan Wagemans, James H. Elder, Michael Kubovy, Stephen E. Palmer, Mary A. Peterson, Manish Singh, and Rudiger von der Heydt, "A century of Gestalt psychology in visual perception: I. Perceptual grouping and figure-ground organization", Psychological Bulletin, volume 138, pages 1172 to 1217, 2012. DOI 10.1037/a0029333. Citation verified against Crossref 19 September 2026. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0029333

## Summary

The standard modern review of perceptual grouping, covering a century of work from Wertheimer onward. The relevant mechanism for interface layout is proximity: elements near each other are grouped before any other property is considered, and proximity generally dominates the competing grouping cues when they conflict. Grouping happens pre-attentively, which is to say the reader has already grouped the page before deciding to read it.

The practical consequence for a screen is that whitespace is not decoration and not a leftover. It is the primary channel that says which pieces belong together, and it operates faster than a border, a heading or a colour. An element placed equidistant between two groups joins neither, or joins the wrong one.

On confidence: high. This is a peer-reviewed review in a top-tier journal with seven authors from the field. The caution is in the transfer: the review is about perception in general, not about comment cards, so it names the mechanism and does not prescribe pixel values.

## Implies for Dialecta

- Comment card (A-5) carries four groups: header row, body, footer row, and the Contrast Strip beneath. Their relationship is communicated by spacing alone, and Dialecta has no spacing token. Whether the Contrast Strip reads as part of the comment or as a separate system observation is decided by a number nobody has chosen deliberately.
- Composer (A-1): the nudge bar sits above the textarea. If the gap above the nudge bar is smaller than the gap below it, the bar groups with whatever is above and stops reading as advice about the box below.
- Classification card (A-3): the structured analysis grid, the commenter message and the self-declaration panel are three groups that arrive in sequence. Proximity is what tells a person whether the self-declaration is a response to the message or a separate task.
- This is the strongest argument for adding a spacing scale to the token set. Space carries meaning here, and meaning that is not tokenised drifts every time a component is built.
