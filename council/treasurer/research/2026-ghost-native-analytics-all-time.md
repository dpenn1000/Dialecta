## Implies for Dialecta

- **The 10-person list has never been emailed.** Two newsletters exist and each was sent to one recipient, presumably Dan testing. The standing recommendation to email the existing list is not a re-engagement play after a lapse. **Nobody on that list has ever received anything from Dialecta.** That makes it cheaper and more promising than this advisor assumed, and it makes the four and a half months of silence entirely self-inflicted.
- **Facebook is the only acquisition channel Dialecta has ever had.** It delivered 54 visitors, a fifth of all traffic and by far the largest non-direct source. Direct at 194 is mostly Dan plus people he told personally, which is the address book already spent. Any organic plan starts from the one channel with evidence behind it, not from a channel somebody likes the idea of.
- **Organic search is not a channel and should be removed from the plan.** Google sent 7 visitors and Bing 2 across five and a half months. The acquisition position listed the five articles working through organic search as a free channel worth having. It is worth 9 visitors. MetaFilter's lesson said do not build the plan on Google, filed in `2014-metafilter-ad-collapse.md`. The measurement says there is nothing there to build on in the first place.
- **The measured conversion rate makes the 16-member target much harder than this advisor said.** At 2.2 percent of visitors becoming real free members, and taking a generous 5 percent free to paid, the visitor-to-paying rate is about 0.11 percent. Sixteen paying members that way needs roughly **14,500 unique visitors**, against 269 all time. That is 54 times the traffic Dialecta has ever had, and it is not a season of writing. The arithmetic is in `../positions/acquisition-cost.md`, corrected.
- **The only path to 16 that survives contact with these numbers runs through direct asking, not through the funnel.** Six real people, asked personally, is a plausible 1 to 3. The remainder has to come from a channel that compounds, and at 5 visitors a week nothing compounds. Dormancy is now quantified: at the current baseline and the measured conversion rate, Dialecta gains about one member every two years.
- The `profile` page drew 44 visitors, third behind the homepage and ahead of every article. People are interested in the identity layer. That is a product finding rather than a treasurer one and the designer should have it.
- Peak traffic of about 68 a week arrived in late April, when Dan was actively publishing and posting to Facebook. Traffic is a function of Dan's activity with no observable floor of its own. **Nothing about this site is yet self-sustaining, including its readership.**
