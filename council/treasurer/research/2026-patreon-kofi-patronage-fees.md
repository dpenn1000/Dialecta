# Patreon and Ko-fi: patronage fee structures

**Source:** Patreon, "Pricing", fetched 2026-09-19, https://www.patreon.com/pricing . Ko-fi's own pages (ko-fi.com/gold and help.ko-fi.com) returned HTTP 403 to automated fetches on the same date; the Ko-fi figures below come from search-result summaries of those pages and are marked unverified.

## Summary

**Patreon (verified against the vendor's page).** The platform fee is "10% of the income you earn on Patreon". The page adds that payment processing, currency conversion and payout fees apply on top, and applicable taxes, but does not break those out. So Patreon's headline rate matches Substack's exactly.

**Ko-fi (not verified against a primary page).** Reported structure: 0 percent platform fee on tips and donations on the free plan, 5 percent on shop sales, memberships and commissions; Ko-fi Gold at about $6/month removes the remaining platform fees. Stripe or PayPal processing applies either way. One secondary source reports Gold may now be $12/month for newer accounts. **Treat every Ko-fi number here as a lead, not a fact.** The reading-list entry for Ko-fi stays open until a primary page can be read.

Patronage differs from membership in what the payer is buying. A membership buys access or standing. Patronage buys the thing continuing to exist. For a platform whose founding brief rules out compromising editorial independence, that distinction is the whole point: patronage has no product to gate and therefore nothing to distort.

## Implies for Dialecta

- Patreon's 10 percent is the same rate as Substack's and it buys less that Dialecta needs, since Dialecta already has its own site, auth and member table. Routing money through Patreon would pay 10 percent for a payment page Stripe provides directly for 2.9 percent plus 30 cents.
- The case for using a patronage platform at all is not the fee. It is discovery and the absence of a Stripe account to administer. Dialecta already has neither problem and already has Stripe in the stack via Ghost members.
- Ko-fi's 0 percent on tips is the only rate in this note that beats direct Stripe, and it is the one figure that could not be verified. Do not build an argument on it.
- The useful import is conceptual: a patronage line ("keep this running") can sit beside a membership line without gating anything, and it is the only revenue shape that survives the charter's veto on models that compromise editorial independence, because nothing is withheld from anyone who does not pay.
