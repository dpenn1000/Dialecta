# Prolific: participant pay and platform fee

**Source:** Prolific, "Pricing", fetched 2026-09-20. https://www.prolific.com/pricing

## Summary

| | Rate |
| --- | --- |
| Minimum participant pay | $8.00/hour (GBP 6.00) |
| Recommended participant pay | at least $12.00/hour (GBP 9.00) |
| Platform fee, standard account | 42.8%, added on top of the reward |
| Platform fee, academic or non-profit account | 33.3%, added on top of the reward |

No minimum spend is stated on the page. Managed services, where Prolific runs the study rather
than just supplying participants, are quote-only and not priced here. "Added on top" means the
participant is paid the full reward; the fee is what the researcher pays in addition, confirmed by
the page's own framing and cross-checked against a worked example on three independent
secondary sources that all reproduce the same 42.8 percent.

Cost of one ten-minute session:

## Implies for Dialecta

- Dialecta has no academic or non-profit standing on file, so the standard 42.8 percent fee is
  the number to plan against. Registering either status later saves about 7 percent of the total,
  not enough to change a recommendation either way.
- The task asks a stranger to write two or three sentences describing a person, not to click
  through a survey. Paying the minimum invites the satisficing a free-text task is most exposed
  to, and a low-effort note is the exact failure mode the reading test exists to catch. Price at
  the recommended rate.
- Prolific recruits and pays participants. It does not build the task surface itself. Showing an
  image, collecting a seven-way match or a written note, and randomising condition still needs a
  page, which is Dan's evenings or an existing free form tool, not a Prolific line item.
