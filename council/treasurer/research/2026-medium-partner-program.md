# Medium Partner Program: the pooled-payout model and what it rewards

**Source:** Medium Help Center, "Calculating earnings in the Partner Program", help.medium.com. The page returned HTTP 403 to automated fetches on 2026-09-19, as did medium.com/creators. The summary below is assembled from search-result extracts of that help page and is **partly unverified**; the structural claim (pooled member subscription, payouts driven by member reading time) is consistent across every source seen and is the claim this note rests on.

## Summary

Medium funds writer payouts from a pooled member subscription of $5/month or $50/year. Writers are paid a share of a monthly payout pool rather than a share of their own readers' payments. The dominant input is **member reading time**: a member read counts when a paying member spends 30 seconds or more on a story. Claps, highlights and replies from members also contribute, and a final adjustment applies the member read ratio, meaning the share of members who loaded the story and then stayed 30 seconds. Reported 2025 additions include bonuses for external traffic, search discovery, email sharing and new member conversions, with 5 percent of the monthly pool set aside for external referrals. Joining requires a paid membership, at least six published stories and a connected Stripe account.

The structure has a property worth naming plainly. When payout is a share of a pool driven by attention metrics, every writer's earnings depend on what other writers do, and the optimisable quantity is time-on-page rather than the quality of the thinking. The measured thing becomes the produced thing.

## Implies for Dialecta

- **This is the model Dialecta must not adopt, and the treasurer's objection is arithmetic before it is philosophical.** A pooled payout makes contributor cost scale linearly with contributors while revenue scales with paying members, which is exactly the shape the charter vetoes. Ten times the contributors splitting the same pool pays each of them a tenth, and the platform has spent nothing less.
- The philosophical objection lands in the same place, which is worth recording because the council usually disagrees. Paying for member reading time rewards the performance of thinking over thinking, and it would turn the Thinking Fingerprint into a scoreboard with a cash value attached. The philosopher's charter vetoes that; so does this advisor's, on cost.
- The read-ratio adjustment is a warning about any Dialecta metric that gets attached to money or status. A 30-second threshold is a target the moment anyone knows it exists. If contributor axes ever carry a reward, the axis becomes the goal.
- What Dialecta can take from Medium is the price point and nothing else: $5/month or $50/year is a recognisable membership price for written work, and the annual number happens to be the one the fee arithmetic already favours. See `2026-stripe-processing-fees.md`.
- The primary help page could not be read automatically. Before this note is cited in a decision, open it in a browser and confirm the payout mechanics have not changed again; they have changed repeatedly since 2017.
