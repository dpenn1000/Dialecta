# Circulation: brief

## Where it is now

The seat was created 2026-09-20 and has filed nothing. Everything below is a starting position to
verify, not a finding.

**What is true about the platform today**, measured rather than estimated, all on 2026-09-20:

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Visitors, all time | 269 |
| Real people among them | 6, of whom 3 are arm's length |
| Published comments | 0. All three sit at `pending_review` |
| Signed-in users | 0. `auth.users` is empty |
| Articles | 5 rows in the live database |
| `apps/web` | 315 lines. The build has barely started |
| Monthly floor | $78, covered by 19 annual memberships at $50 |

**The site is not ready to be visited and Dan has decided it will be before anyone is invited.**
That is the sequencing this seat works inside. It buys time to do the measurement and the
groundwork properly rather than reactively.

## Next three

1. **Correct the funnel.** Establish what the 269 and the 6 actually consist of, where they came
   from, and what conclusion can honestly be drawn from a site with nothing published. Say plainly
   what the 17,000-visitor figure is worth. This is the first thing the Council needs.
2. **The share surface.** A shared Dialecta article is the whole strategy. Work out what it
   carries: the claim, the tier badge, the fingerprint, an excerpt, and what each of those does to
   a stranger's decision to click. `legal` holds whether a badge about a named person may travel
   off-platform at all, and `designer` owns the card. Open the question with both.
3. **Where Dialecta's readers already are.** Not "which platform is biggest". Which platform
   carries long-form argument to people who read it, and what a piece has to look like there.

## Standing questions this seat inherits

- Whether paid acquisition returns anything at this scale, argued with arithmetic against
  `treasurer` rather than asserted.
- Whether author recruitment is a distribution problem or an editorial one. Probably both, and the
  split matters.
- What the founding essay's indictment of social media rules out for this seat specifically.
  `philosopher` will hold that line and should.
