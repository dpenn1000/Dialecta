# Kelly's arithmetic assumes arrival; it does not explain it

**Source:** Same primary source as `council/treasurer/research/2008-kelly-1000-true-fans.md`
(Kevin Kelly, "1,000 True Fans", The Technium, originally published March 2008, edited 2014
version). Read there per the reading list. This note covers what the essay says, and does not
say, about how a fan is found in the first place, which is outside treasurer's remit and inside
mine.

## Summary

Treasurer's note carries the revenue arithmetic: Dialecta needs roughly six Kelly-sized true fans,
or twelve $50 memberships, not a thousand of anything. That stands.

What the essay does not cover, and does not claim to, is how a creator finds candidate fans before
the direct relationship exists. Kelly's model starts from a creator who can already reach an
audience, however small, and describes how to deepen that relationship into $100-a-year support.
It is silent on cold discovery. Read for arrival rather than revenue, the essay's real
contribution is narrower than it is usually quoted for: it argues for owning the relationship, a
mailing list, a direct line, once someone has found you. It does not argue for how they find you.

## Implies for Dialecta

- Dialecta already has the owned channel Kelly's model wants: a Resend audience of 10 people. Per
  treasurer's Ghost analytics note, it has never been emailed. That is the one piece of Kelly's
  arithmetic Dialecta could act on this week for zero new cost, and it is the reason
  `2026-resend-broadcasts-tool.md` is filed alongside this note.
- Do not stretch this source to cover acquisition. It is tempting, because it is the most
  encouraging number in the research tree, but the essay earns its place on the monetization
  shelf, not this one. Marking that limit plainly is worth more than borrowing authority the
  source does not have.
