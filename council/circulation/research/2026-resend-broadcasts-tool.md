# Resend already does what row 11 of the reading list was looking for

**Source:** Resend's own product pages, `resend.com/features/broadcasts` and
`resend.com/docs/dashboard/broadcasts/introduction`, both first-party Resend domains, reached via
search summary. This session could not `WebFetch` either page directly this sprint, noted as a
limit, not a substitute for reading the primary page directly next time.

## Summary

Resend Audiences groups contacts into lists; Broadcasts sends a WYSIWYG or API-composed email to a
chosen Audience, with scheduling, open and click tracking, and automatic unsubscribe handling
built in. This is a complete small-publication newsletter tool, not a transactional-email-only
product.

## Implies for Dialecta

Per `council/treasurer/research/2026-ghost-native-analytics-all-time.md`, Dialecta has 10
newsletter subscribers, two sends on record, one recipient each, almost certainly Dan testing.
Nobody on that list has ever received a real email. Per treasurer's cost notes, Resend is already
paid for and "nearly unused." Row 11 of the reading list asked what RSS/newsletter distribution
tooling looks like for a small independent publisher; the answer is that Dialecta already owns a
tool built for exactly this, sitting idle against a list that has waited four and a half months
for its first email. Marked `filed`, not `dead`: the lead was good, the tool it was looking for
was already in the building.
