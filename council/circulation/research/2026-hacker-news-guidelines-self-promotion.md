# Hacker News: the largest single arrival event that cannot be bought, and cannot be asked for either

**Source:** Hacker News official guidelines, news.ycombinator.com/newsguidelines.html, primary,
fetched 2026-09-20.

## Summary

The guidelines are explicit and narrow. Self-promotion is limited, not banned: "It's ok to post
your own stuff part of the time, but the primary use of the site should be for curiosity."
Soliciting votes, comments or submissions is flatly prohibited: "Don't solicit upvotes, comments,
or submissions." Accounts are expected to carry a stable identity ("HN is a community, users
should have an identity that others can relate to"); routine throwaways are discouraged. Titles
may not be dressed up: no caps, no exclamation points, no editorialising about how good the piece
is, and a "gratuitous number or number + adjective" in a title is something the guidelines ask
submitters to crop.

**What the guidelines do not state: how the ranking algorithm works, or what specifically drives
front-page placement.** That is not documented publicly at this address, and this note does not
claim otherwise. The reading list's premise, that a front-page appearance is the largest available
arrival event and "cannot be bought," is confirmed only in the narrow sense that HN has no
paid-submission product and vote-buying is explicitly against the rules. It is not confirmed by
any public statement about ranking mechanics.

## Implies for Dialecta

- The one move this seat might otherwise reach for, asking Dialecta's existing list or personal
  network to go upvote a submission, is exactly what the guidelines name and ban. Worth stating
  plainly since it is the natural instinct for a one-operator publication with six known real
  people and no other audience yet.
- HN suits Dialecta's actual content, long-form argument, better than Facebook does in principle.
  But an account submitting a brand-new, unknown domain reads as the self-promotion pattern the
  guidelines single out, and there is no reliable second attempt if a submission flops or gets
  flagged, unlike a channel that can be tried weekly at no cost.
- Recommend HN as a second-stage channel, tried once the site is genuinely finished per Dan's own
  sequencing, and better submitted by a genuine reader who found the piece worth sharing than by
  Dan's own account, which is a harder thing to arrange than it is to state. Not where the first
  hour goes; see `positions.md`.
- Reddit's self-promotion rules are the same shape (reading list row 7) and remain untouched this
  sprint; carried forward as `todo`.
