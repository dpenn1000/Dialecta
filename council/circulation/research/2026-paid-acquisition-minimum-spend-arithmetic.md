# Paid acquisition's minimum viable spend, against a $78 floor

**Source:** Google's own Ads Help, support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6312 (primary, fetched
2026-09-20, confirms no stated minimum budget on that page). A minimum for Demand Gen campaigns
specifically ($5/day, effective April 2026) is reported by Search Engine Land, citing Google's own
Ads Developer Blog; that blog post did not return fetchable content to this session and is cited
at one remove, not independently verified. Meta's own Business Help Center pages returned no
fetchable content to this session; Meta figures below are relayed via vendor and agency blogs
(stackmatix.com, tryvizup.com, get-ryze.ai and others), marked as such throughout, since this
field is mostly written by people selling ad-management services.

## Summary

**Google:** no platform-wide minimum spend; a campaign can technically run for $1/day. A narrower
rule applies only to Demand Gen campaigns from April 2026: a $5/day floor, reported as intended to
give Google's own optimisation enough signal to function. Multiple vendor sources converge on
$1,000 to $2,500 a month as the practical floor for a small business campaign to generate the
roughly 30 conversions a period needed before Smart Bidding has enough data to optimise; below
that, spend buys clicks without buying the platform's own learning loop.

**Meta:** vendor sources report official technical minimums of $1/day for impression-based
campaigns and $5/day for campaigns optimising toward clicks or conversions, unverified against
Meta's own page. The same sources converge on $50 to $150 a day as the practical floor, citing
Meta's delivery system needing roughly 50 conversion events per ad set per week to exit its
learning phase.

**Arithmetic against Dialecta's own numbers.** Even at the smallest official minimums, a month of
any campaign costs $30 to $150, against a $78 monthly floor Dialecta has never covered organically.
At the practical floors vendor sources describe as necessary for the platforms' own algorithms to
work as designed, a single month of Google spend ($1,000+) or Meta spend ($1,500 to $4,500+) costs
many times Dialecta's entire operating budget, to reach a conversion rate that
`2026-dialecta-funnel-correction.md` establishes is currently unmeasured for real cold traffic,
not merely low.

## Implies for Dialecta

- **This is an acquisition-cost finding, not a revenue one. It does not touch treasurer's veto on
  advertising as income, and it is kept apart from that question by design.** The answer here is
  that paid acquisition is not worth spending on yet, for arithmetic reasons specific to scale,
  not because advertising is categorically wrong for a discourse platform.
- The honest threshold: paid acquisition becomes worth evaluating once Dialecta can point real,
  converting, real-discourse traffic at an ad in numbers that make even the technical minimum ($30
  to $150 a month) a rounding error rather than a multiple of the floor. That is not close today.
- Recommend the free, already-proven channel, Facebook, see `2026-dialecta-funnel-correction.md`,
  run for at least one full month before any paid dollar is spent anywhere, purely so there is a
  real baseline conversion number to compare a paid channel's cost against.
