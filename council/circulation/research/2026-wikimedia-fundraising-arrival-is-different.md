# Wikimedia's funnel starts where Dialecta's problem is

**Source:** Same primary source as `council/treasurer/research/2026-wikimedia-fundraising.md`
(Wikimedia Foundation audited financial statements FY2024-2025, meta.wikimedia.org). Read there
per the reading list. This note states the limit treasurer's summary does not need to state:
what Wikimedia's numbers require to already be true before any of them apply.

## Summary

Treasurer's note is right that the $11 average gift and the 21 percent recurring-giving share are
the legible small-donor benchmarks at this seat's scale. What it does not need to state, because
it is not a treasurer question, is where Wikimedia's askable audience comes from: people arrive at
Wikipedia because they searched for a fact, not because they were persuaded to. The banner appears
to someone already on the page for an unrelated reason, in enormous volume, for free, because
Wikipedia is reference infrastructure with no substitute most searchers think to reach for.

Dialecta is not that. It is an opinion and discourse destination competing for attention on
purpose, not utility. Nothing about Wikimedia's fundraising mechanics explains how to generate the
arrival the banner depends on, because Wikimedia has never had to solve that problem.

## Implies for Dialecta

- Use Wikimedia for the ask design, recurring over one-off, the average-gift framing, exactly as
  treasurer does. Do not use it as evidence about acquisition. It has none to offer, and its own
  scale makes that easy to miss.
- This is the same caution as the 1,000 True Fans note, from the opposite direction: a source that
  answers the revenue question well can look like it answers the arrival question too, and on
  these two, it does not.
