# How to read one of these marks

Every contributor on this site has one. It is built from their whole record and cannot be edited.

**Six pillars, at fixed angles, clockwise from the top.** Every mark uses the same six angles.

| Angle | Pillar | What it measures |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0, straight up | Acuity | How precise and claimable the points tend to be |
| 60, upper right | Calibration | How well stated confidence matches the evidence behind it |
| 120, lower right | Magnanimity | How faithfully opposing views are represented first |
| 180, straight down | Discourse | Sustained back and forth rather than hit and run |
| 240, lower left | Consistency | How regularly the person shows up |
| 300, upper left | Reach | How many distinct subject areas they write in |

**How far the boundary reaches at an angle is how much that pillar has been earned.** A long reach
toward the upper left means many subjects. A boundary pulled in toward the centre at the lower
right means little has been earned on Magnanimity. Nothing is ever subtracted; a pillar grows only
by being earned, so a short one means not yet rather than punished.

**The dashed circle is a horizon.** It is drawn at the same radius on every mark and nobody reaches it.

**Ring colour is the subject, laid down outward in time.** The centre holds the oldest work and the
rim the most recent, so reading from the middle outward reads a career in order. A mark that runs
one colour at the core and another at the rim is a person who changed subject.

**A wobbling or jagged boundary is heat.** Arguments that ran hot leave the line unsettled. Heat
settles as the record grows, so old heat shows near the centre and recent heat at the rim.

**Duller, greyer colour** on part of the mark means a mixed record there. Full colour means a clean one.

**The outer glow is reception**, how much other people engaged. Its hue is the subject written about most.

**Overall size is roughly total earned history**, which grows with time spent on the site.
