# How to read one of these marks

Every contributor on this site has one. It is built from their whole record and cannot be edited.
One small mark is deposited for every comment they have ever written, and the outline is just the
edge of the pile.

**Six pillars, at fixed angles, clockwise from the top.** A comment sits at the angle of whichever
pillar it moved.

| Angle | Pillar | What it measures |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0, straight up | Acuity | How precise and claimable the points tend to be |
| 60, upper right | Calibration | How well stated confidence matches the evidence behind it |
| 120, lower right | Magnanimity | How faithfully opposing views are represented first |
| 180, straight down | Discourse | Sustained back and forth rather than hit and run |
| 240, lower left | Consistency | How regularly the person shows up |
| 300, upper left | Reach | How many distinct subject areas they write in |

**How far out a mark sits is when it happened.** The centre is the beginning, the rim is now.
Reading from the middle outward reads a career in order.

**Colour is the subject** that comment was about.

**Form is the kind of comment.** A plain dot is ordinary good work. A dot with a pale core is a
sharp one. A hollow ring is agreement adding nothing. A soft smudge is vague. A short radial
stroke is heat, an argument that ran hot. A short bar across the radius is a position held
regardless of the evidence.

**How densely packed the marks are is how fast the person writes.** A prolific month packs marks
into the same band rather than pushing the edge outward.

**Overall size is time on the site**, not volume.
