## What the sprints found

**Sprint 1, behaviour.** No precedent filed gates the act of writing a first comment. Discourse
gates reach, Stack Overflow gates authority over other people's posts, Community Notes gates
publication, MetaFilter gates identity and time. None gates length. Separately, Supabase's own docs
say the built-in email provider sends two per hour and refuses delivery outside the project team, so
magic link sign-up cannot work at cutover on defaults.

**Sprint 2, craft.** Six measured failures in the locked tier palette, reproducible from
`council/designer/research/tier-palette-audit.py`. Heat badge text at 1.96:1 where 4.5:1 is needed,
Stance at 4.26:1, Stance and Breach borders 9.22 CIEDE2000 apart and closer under every simulated
colour vision deficiency, Forum border at 1.50:1 against the card. The one contrast fix already in
the Discourse Layer UX spec was applied to Forum, which measures 6.39:1 and did not need it.
Separately, the token set defines no spacing value, no type size, no line height and no measure,
and the design spec uses 29 font sizes and 17 padding values in their place.

**Sprint 3, repositories.** The best finds were Dan's own. `dpenn1000/trinity-platform` carries a
finished design system whose spacing standard solves D-15, including a direction rule this advisor
did not have, and whose colour law already forbids the thing the Heat badge does.
`dpenn1000/dialecta-api` carries five article endpoints this repo lacks, including
`aesthetic-suggest.js`. Externally, Radix Colors supplied the one idea that turns the palette audit
into something fixable.
