# The rendered fingerprint, checked against its own intent

*philosopher position, 2026-09-20. Council review of the live Fingerprint page at Dan's request,
against `Dialecta_Contributor_Identity.md`, `Dialecta_Founding_Philosophy.md`,
`Dialecta_Tier_Psychology.md`, ADR-004 and both its amendments, and my own P-9, P-11, and
`2026-09-20-visibility-control.md`.*

## Brief

The record-not-score claim holds in the architecture: unfakeable, built from tier history, texture
that remembers regression by design. It does not fully hold in the render. The dotted circle earns
its claim, labeled "the potential ring" in the code and reached by almost no one: horizon, not
ceiling. The concave notch does not. A bite in a shape reads as a wound before anyone parses which
axis is low, the shame-read Tier Psychology avoided at the naming layer. ADR-004's residual is
reasoned well alone; beside an unplanned wound it risks reading as two wounds, not one. P-11 is more
urgent now: what has no contest path is a face, not a number. Good, with one repair, not a rebuild.

## Position

**1. Record vs. score.** Real in the architecture, insecure in the render. The computation is
unfakeable and historical; regression stays visible on purpose. A shape read whole, before any axis
is named, uses the same grammar as a dashboard gauge: scorecard first, record second. It needs the
viewer's literacy to land as a record.

**2. The notch.** Tier Psychology rejected "The Gutter" for Breach because visceral language risks
shame ("you're trashy") over accountability ("you crossed a line"), a deliberate choice at the
naming layer. The notch reintroduces that risk at the shape layer, undecided: no spec chose
concavity as meaningful, it's what a radar chart does to a low value by default. A smaller petal
reads "not yet." A dent pulled inward reads as damage. Per my own P-1, unsettled: the platform
already worked to avoid this once, now unexamined where a viewer looks first.

**3. The dotted circle.** This one earns its claim. Named "the potential ring" in the code from zero
comments; the newborn state calls it "the potential of who you could become." Almost no shape
reaches it; the one demo archetype that touches the rim does so on a single axis, by design.
Equidistant from everyone, it reads as horizon, not reproach: nobody's shortfall is measured against
a peer who cleared it. It does what the text says. Its weakness: "potential" lives in onboarding
copy, not the profile page a returning contributor sees.

**4. P-11, seeing it.** Less alarming in one respect: this isn't a scoreboard aesthetic, and the
circle earns the benefit of the doubt I withheld on paper. More urgent where it matters: P-11 named
an abstract gap, "no contest mechanism." Seeing the shape shows what that gap protects: not a data
point in a table, but the instant, whole impression of a person, read before any axis is parsed. The
gap is the same size. What it guards just got a face.

**5. ADR-004 on this shape.** Dan's reasoning holds on its own: proportionate, diluting, no timer,
one bad day doesn't dye the shape. Article 4, done right. The risk is compounding, not the residual:
a deliberate wound-signal landing on a shape that may carry an unplanned one at its low axis. Two
sound decisions can read as a shape hurt twice, only once on purpose. `designer` already owns this
geometry; resolving it alongside the notch, in one pass, beats shipping both separately.

**6. Is it good as it is?** Yes. The architecture is the most coherent thing in this project:
unfakeable, texture that remembers on purpose, potential rendered as horizon rather than ceiling.
The specs anticipated the hard case, visible regression, and chose honesty over comfort. It needs a
finishing pass, not a rebuild: carry "potential" onto the ordinary render, and let one designer
resolve the notch and the residual together before either ships wider.
