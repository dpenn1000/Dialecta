# The fingerprint, read by a stranger

*philosopher position, 2026-09-20, for `council/log/2026-09-20-fingerprint-legibility-and-model.md`.
Numbers are rerun tonight from that folder or taken from the file named beside them. New sources:

## Brief

The good, bad and ugly can be shown without shame if the bad is drawn as dated acts, and never as a
texture of the whole person. The ring model draws tier history as undated texture, and its noise
floor wears heat's grammar. Strongest evidence: Father Anselm's record holds zero Heat and zero
Stance, and a stranger's note on his mark ends "lately their arguments have been getting heated." The
page's showcase draws the Reviser, the archetype defined by change, with no Heat or Stance, where the
spec calls for earlier heat resolving. The renderer should read events, the trade-off penalty should
go, and no ordinal mark should sit beside a vote, where it feeds the tier it displays.

## The model

A person's comments laid down in the order written, so that what they wrote about, how they argued
and when either changed all read from the centre out, and every mark that judges counts back to the
comments that made it. Three things built contradict that. In order of harm:

1. **The noise floor on the heat channel.** The live carousel runs at turbulence zero by
   construction and still waves, under copy saying turbulence makes the waves.
2. **The trade-off penalty.** Delete it, with Dan.
3. **Undated tiers.** `axis_events` already holds a tier and a date per event. The renderer should
   read it.

## The good, bad and ugly

Tangney, Stuewig and Mashek (2007): shame judges the global self and goes with hiding, defensiveness
and anger turned outward; guilt judges a specific behaviour and goes with apology and repair. Stable
attributions for a failure go with shame. Tier Psychology's first principle, describe the comment and
not the person, is that finding in words. A dated act inside a larger record reads as a scar, which
the page promises: "the learning scar of someone who arrived writing from feeling and figured out how
to reason." An undated texture across the whole shape reads as a wound, and it is the only way the
ring model draws tier history.

All seven single-mark readers reported heat, the four heated records and the three calm ones alike,
so the reading carried no information. Of the bad the mark shows, Anselm's wobble is the renderer's:
noise at 1.6 percent of his radius at the rim and 9.6 at the core, over a heat term of zero. What
the penalty subtracts is the platform's. Only dated acts are the person's. A judging channel needs a
zero that looks like zero, which a count has and an organic line never will. Set the human study's
bar now: a heat reading at least ten times likelier on a record with heat than on one without.
Tonight, on seven notes, the ratio is one.

## The trade-off penalty

The penalty is the record editorialising, and it has already rewritten the specs. Dolores Vance holds
22 graduations on Discourse, tied for her highest; drawn at 70 percent it ranks fifth, and her
stranger named it her weakest pillar. The archetype grid says the pairs "are respected," and its data
shows the cost. The spec's Synthesizer pairs Reach with Acuity, which it calls "rare and visible," and
the showcase drops her Acuity to 11. The spec's Illuminator has "high Discourse," and the showcase
gives her 8, her lowest. Dan exempted Consistency because penalising it "would have the platform say
that showing up reliably costs you something elsewhere" (`fingerprint-geometry.ts`). The penalty says
that about fairness, to each person, including those whose record contradicts it. His exemption
assumed the penalty stays, so its deletion is his to make.

## Dan's six properties

| Property | Where it stands |
| --- | --- |
| No two the same | Holds for seven people written to differ, untested on a crowd. Part of it is seeded noise, the noise that accused Anselm. Uniqueness should be earned, so two empty records may look alike |
| Personal identity | Holds on the profile. Beside a vote it becomes rank |
| The good, bad and ugly | Holds only as dated acts with a visible zero. Fails today |
| Grows, never changed | Fails. 30 of 210 one-month earning events pull another petal inward, by up to 4.5px. Deletion cascades, so a person can prune. How someone argues has no dates |
| Organic | Holds, and costs nothing where it means nothing. The carousel halo Dan liked is one hue, 84 to 85, on all three marks |
| Readable at a glance | 19 of 21 at 330px, 5 of 21 at 64px. Part of the 330px result is luck: reverse Dolores's history, calm first and heated late, and her mark does not move |

Organic collides with readable only on a shared channel, and wobble is one: the legend calls it heat
and the noise floor gives it to everyone. "Can never be changed" borrows from the wrong object. A
fingerprint is fixed before birth and records nothing its owner did; tree rings keep every layer and
add new ones outward, which is what Dan describes and what the render draws. Dilution is growth. The
penalty is revision, and so is `designer`'s Breach clock, which ADR-004 ruled out as "a timer anyone
has to tune." A residual that fades while its owner is away forgives by lapse. Drop the clock or amend
the ADR.

## Scoreboard and contest path

A readable mark is not the scoreboard my charter vetoes. A readable mark beside a vote is. At 64px the
ring carries size alone, and size has a better end. The comment card already shows the author's
archetype on the same card as the vote controls, and community voting is 35 percent of a comment's
classification. Tomkins, Zhang and Heavlin (2017) gave each paper two reviewers who could see its
authors and two who could not; seeing raised the odds of a recommendation to accept 1.63 times for
famous authors, among experts. A byline mark makes the vote a single-blind review of the person, and
the vote grows the mark. `designer`'s thread sketch sells knowing the veterans "before reading a
word." Article 6 protects difference "without turning difference into hierarchy." In the thread: one
size for everyone, subject hue at most, the full mark a tap away. Recognition survives, and the
product idea with it.

P-11 changes grain. None of Anselm's comments is misclassified, so re-reviewing all of them would
leave the mark accusing him. A render error has nothing to contest. Its remedy is a count anyone can
check beside the mark: Heat, none.

## What Dan is missing

- The mark records what a person wrote about over time, and not how they argued over time. The
  spec's Reviser carries "visible wave texture from earlier tiers resolving into Forum"; the showcase
  gives her Heat 0 and Stance 0, and no tier in the model has a date to resolve from.
- The stranger is a reader no spec has written for. The Editorial Voice keeps "personality labels"
  and "ranking against other people" out of a contributor's view of their own fingerprint, and has no
  row for anyone else's. A personality label is what a stranger takes away. The Voice needs that
  row, carrying at least the self-view's exclusions.
- Legibility raises the price of heat, and the delete cascade lets a person pay it by pruning. Settle
  the cascade before the mark goes public, or the record becomes a portfolio.

## Rebuttal

`spec-reader` quotes the strongest point against me. Contributor Identity says the trade-offs
"are not bugs": they make "a shape rather than a uniform circle," and if everyone could max every
pillar, "the pillars meant nothing." My high confidence that the penalty contradicts the specs
was misplaced. It obeys that paragraph and breaks the sentence above it that calls high Acuity
with high Reach "rare and visible."

The penalty caps only the drawing; `fingerprint-geometry.test.ts` names the case
"holds when a real contributor maxes every axis." Ported, the formula draws every record with 44
or more on all six, whatever the mix, within 0.05px of one shape at a 118px horizon: 0.80 on four
pillars, 0.70 on Discourse, 1.00 on Consistency. Past the horizon the silhouette separates nobody
either way; the penalty picks the shape they share.

The document should change, by Dan's hand. Its bullets state tendencies a record will show
("tends to outrun," "will accumulate some Heat"). Its last paragraph makes them the cause of
every shape; that claim should go.

`designer` would retire the penalty too, last of nine, through an envelope. Deletion needs none.
Setting `TRADEOFF_PENALTY = 0.2` to zero and updating its tests loses nothing the six totals hold.
Ranked last, the penalty ships in the first self-visible render, so the first person shown
Discourse fifth of six on a record like Dolores's, where it ties for first, is its owner.

`legal` is right that a drawing can be contested; my "nothing to contest" was wrong. The counts it
hands me belong on the profile, Heat and Stance by season, dating acts; beside a vote they rank
people. Keep `security`'s three-comment floor on the public mark, whatever it buys privacy: drawn
from two comments, a mark makes two acts the whole person.
