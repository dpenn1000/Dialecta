# Young children, minimal groups, and dichotomous categorization

**Source:** Spielman, D. A. (2000). "Young Children, Minimal Groups, and Dichotomous Categorization." Personality and Social Psychology Bulletin, 26(11), 1433-1441. DOI 10.1177/0146167200263010 https://doi.org/10.1177/0146167200263010 (abstract read verbatim through the Crossref record)

## Summary

Two experiments test whether minimal-group bias depends on a dichotomous split rather than on categorization as such. Experiment 1: children in two-group or three-group conditions, with no prime, a neutral prime, or a competitive prime, showed no in-group bias in either condition unless competitively primed. Experiment 2: undergraduates showed bias in two-group contexts, and in three-group contexts only when competitively primed.

## Limits

Minimal groups again: assigned, contentless, a reward-allocation task. The adult sample is undergraduates. Abstract-level reading.
