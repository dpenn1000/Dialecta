# Preventing harassment and increasing group participation through social norms in 2,190 online science discussions

**Source:** Matias, J. N. (2019). "Preventing harassment and increasing group participation through social norms in 2,190 online science discussions." Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences, 116(20), 9785-9789. DOI 10.1073/pnas.1813486116 https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.1813486116

## Summary

A field experiment in r/science (13 million subscribers) randomized whether a visible statement of community rules appeared at the top of a discussion. Displaying the rules, with no change to what was actually permitted or enforced, increased newcomer rule compliance by more than 8 percentage points and increased the rate of newcomer participation by 70 percent on average. The mechanism was the visibility of the norm, not a change in what could be posted or a barrier to posting it; the same discussion, same moderation, same allowed content, produced more and better-behaved participation once the expectation was stated where a newcomer would see it before writing.

## Implies for Dialecta

- This is the evidence `designer` asked for in `exchange/open/2026-09-19-003-blindspot-composer-gate-against-first-comment.md`: a low-friction signal of what is expected can do real cultural work, measured, at newcomer scale. The claim designer could see the shape of but not source now has a source.
- The finding is specific about *which* mechanism carries the effect, and it is not the composer's 12-character gate. Matias's intervention was an announcement, present and readable before the person wrote anything, imposing no functional barrier to posting. Dialecta already has this instrument: the Stage 1 nudge bar. This is evidence for investing in the nudge bar's copy under Editorial Voice v1.2, not for keeping a disabled button.
- A blocking gate and a visible-norm statement are not two strengths of the same mechanism, they are different mechanisms, and only one of them has a field result behind it. A character-count floor blocks silently; it does not state a norm, it enforces an unstated one, which is the opposite of what produced Matias's effect.
- Bears on the Pact and onboarding as much as on the composer, since the same logic (state the expectation visibly, do not gate on it) applies to sign-up.
