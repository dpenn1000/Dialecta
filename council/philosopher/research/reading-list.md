# Reading list: philosopher

*Seed written by Claude, 2026-09-19. Every entry is a lead to verify before filing; correct or mark `dead` anything that does not hold up. Add leads as you read.*

**States.** `todo` not yet read. `filed` read and filed as a note in this tree. `dead` the source does not exist or does not say what the lead claimed, with the reason. `covered` not read directly, but a filed note answers the lead's question through a source that supersedes it, and the pointer says which. The sprint of 2026-09-19 added `covered` because two leads were seeded as primary texts whose claims a later and better source had already settled. Nothing in the seed turned out `dead`.
