# Shifting attention to accuracy can reduce misinformation online

**Source:** Pennycook, G., Epstein, Z., Mosleh, M., Arechar, A. A., Eckles, D., and Rand, D. G. (2021). "Shifting attention to accuracy can reduce misinformation online." Nature, 592(7855), 590-595. DOI 10.1038/s41586-021-03344-2 https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-021-03344-2

## Summary

Four survey experiments and one field experiment on Twitter. The veracity of a headline had little effect on whether people intended to share it, although it had a large effect on whether they judged it accurate. Most participants nonetheless said that sharing only accurate content mattered to them. The authors resolve the contradiction with an inattention account: the preference for accuracy is real and held, but it is not applied at the moment of sharing because attention is on other things. Subtly shifting attention to accuracy raised the quality of the news participants shared afterwards. The authors present this as evidence against the claim that people value partisanship over accuracy, and as support for attention based interventions that platforms could deploy at scale.

## Implies for Dialecta

- This is the closest published analogue to the classification card, and it locates the mechanism precisely. The card works, to the extent it works, by putting attention on quality at the moment of posting. Not by teaching, not by scoring, and not by accumulating a fingerprint.
- That makes the card's position in the flow the load bearing design choice. Stage 2 of the Discourse Layer sits between writing and posting, which is the right place. Any proposal to move classification feedback later, into a notification, the profile, or a digest, surrenders the mechanism this study identifies.
- The inattention account is a friendly amendment to the source thesis in the Founding Philosophy. The thesis says environments shape behavior more than stated values do. Pennycook et al. find the stated value intact and the environment defeating it by directing attention elsewhere. The design consequence differs: Dialecta does not have to install a standard contributors lack, it has to stop the moment of posting from crowding out one they already hold. That is a smaller claim, and it is the one with evidence under it.
