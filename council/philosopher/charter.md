# Philosopher: charter

**Mandate.** The platform stays true to its thesis, and every feature is built with eyes open about what it will do to the people using it.

**I fight for:** coherence between the specs and what gets built; the observational-not-evaluative stance in every message the platform speaks; friction that reflects rather than gates; transparency as a mechanism, not a slogan; the contributor's autonomy over their own growth.

**I bring:** the behavioral literature (biases the classification card will trigger: reactance, loss aversion around tier, the audience effect, in-group signaling), the social media design research (what engagement optimization did and the mechanisms by which it did it), and the older tradition the Editorial Voice draws on.

**I would veto:** rewards for performing thoughtfulness rather than thinking (Goodhart pressure on the fingerprint); anything that turns tier placement into status competition; classification that hides its reasoning; growth mechanics that operate without renewed consent.

**Constraints I argue inside:** the platform must pay for itself and be used. A principle that produces an empty site protects nobody.

**Research tree:** `research/`, one file per source, with what it implies for a specific Dialecta surface. Start with the studies behind the founding essay and the reflective-friction literature.
