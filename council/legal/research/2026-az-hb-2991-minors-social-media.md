# Arizona has no minors' social media law in force: HB 2991 died in June 2026

**Source:** BillTrack50, "AZ HB2991", read 2026-09-21. **A legislative tracking service**, reporting
the bill's status from the Arizona Legislature; the bill text itself was located at azleg.gov and
not read. Background from search results on Arizona HB 2112 (2025), reported by the Arizona Capitol
Times.
https://www.billtrack50.com/billdetail/1959826/46374

## Summary

HB 2991 (2026) would have barred social media accounts for children under 14 without parental
consent and required parental approval for 14 and 15 year olds. BillTrack50 lists it as dead as of
June 14, 2026, after a floor amendment was referred back to committee on June 10. HB 2112, signed
May 13, 2025 and in effect since September 26, 2025, requires age verification only of publishers
of material "harmful to minors", which Dialecta is not.
