# CalOPPA: a privacy policy owed by any commercial site with California users, at any size

**Source:** Cal. Bus. and Prof. Code Sections 22575 and 22577, California Legislative Information,
read 2026-09-21 through the fetch tool's extraction of the code sections. Primary text.
https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=BPC&sectionNum=22575
https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=BPC&sectionNum=22577

## Summary

**Who:** "An operator of a commercial Web site or online service that collects personally
identifiable information through the Internet about individual consumers residing in California".
No revenue or volume threshold. An operator owns the site and collects for commercial purposes. A
consumer is an individual who "seeks or acquires, by purchase or lease, any goods, services, money,
or credit for personal, family, or household purposes." Personally identifiable information
includes name and email address and "any other identifier that permits the physical or online
contacting of a specific individual."

**What the policy must do:** identify the categories of information collected and "the
categories of third-party persons or entities with whom the operator may share" it; describe any
process for reviewing and requesting changes; describe how consumers are notified of material
changes; state its effective date; "disclose how the operator responds to Web browser 'do not
track' signals"; and "disclose whether other parties may collect personally identifiable
information about an individual consumer's online activities over time and across different Web
sites".

**Conspicuous posting:** on the home page, or through a link containing the word "privacy" that
stands out, or "any other functional hyperlink that is so displayed that a reasonable person
would notice it."

  sale to a Californian.** It corrects nothing in the standing privacy positions, which were about
