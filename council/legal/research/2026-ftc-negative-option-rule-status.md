# The FTC click-to-cancel rule is vacated, and what replaced it is a question, not a rule

**Source:** Three reads, 2026-09-20. The Eighth Circuit vacatur is reported by Latham and Watkins,
WilmerHale and Steptoe, all named firms, converging on the same date and ground. The restart is
reported by Gibson Dunn and Cooley and confirmed against the Federal Register notice itself and
the FTC's own press release.

https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2026/03/ftc-seeks-public-comment-response-advance-notice-proposed-rulemaking-regarding-negative-option

## Summary, with dates

| Date | What happened |
| --- | --- |
| 2024-11-15 | FTC's amended Negative Option Rule, the "click-to-cancel" rule, published |
| 2025-07-08 | Eighth Circuit vacated it in full, on the FTC's failure to follow a mandatory procedural step. Days before the compliance deadline |
| 2026-03-11 | FTC announced an Advance Notice of Proposed Rulemaking |
| 2026-03-13 | ANPRM published in the Federal Register |
| 2026-04-13 | Comments closed |
| 2026-09-20 | No notice of proposed rulemaking has issued. There is no proposed regulatory text |

The vacated rule left the pre-2024 Negative Option Rule standing, which governs prenotification
plans, the book-of-the-month structure where a seller ships unless the consumer declines. That is
not a paid subscription and it does not reach Dialecta.

**What did not change.** ROSCA, FTC Act Section 5 and the state automatic renewal laws are
untouched by the vacatur and are fully operative. Gibson Dunn's framing is that enforcement under
ROSCA continues; the FTC sued JustAnswer on ROSCA grounds on 2026-01-13 and Uber earlier.

## Implies for Dialecta

- **Do not design to the click-to-cancel rule, and do not ignore what it asked for.** It is not
  law. Its substantive asks, disclosure before billing details, separate consent, cancellation as
  easy as sign-up, are either already ROSCA duties or already state law, and an NPRM could restore
  the rest with a compliance date attached.
- **The practical read for a one-operator site: build to ROSCA plus California plus New York and
  the federal rule becomes a non-event whichever way it lands.** Those three are stricter than the
  vacated rule on the points that matter here.
- The rulemaking is worth one check a year, not a watch. An NPRM would carry its own comment
  period and compliance date, so there is no scenario where a duty appears without warning.
- Not legal advice. The vacatur decision itself was not read; this note rests on four named firms
  agreeing on its date and ground.
