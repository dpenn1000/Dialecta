# Cal. Civ. Code 1798.140(ae): sensitive personal information, read for the same question

**Source:** California Civil Code Section 1798.140(ae), as amended by the CPRA, primary text via
the Legislature's own site. Read 2026-09-21. A new row, not previously on `research/reading-list.md`;
added there implicitly by this note's filing.
https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=1798.140.&lawCode=CIV

## Summary

**1798.140(ae)(1)(D).** "Sensitive personal information" includes "a consumer's racial or ethnic
origin, citizenship or immigration status, religious or philosophical beliefs, or union
membership." Listed alongside precise geolocation, genetic data, biometric data used for
identification, health data, and sex life or sexual orientation data elsewhere in the same
subsection (not quoted here; this note only confirms the religious-belief clause).

**What follows from the label, and what does not.** Unlike Article 9 of the GDPR, a "sensitive
personal information" label under the CCPA does not itself prohibit processing. It triggers a
consumer's right to limit the use and disclosure of that category and a business's obligation to
post a "Limit the Use of My Sensitive Personal Information" link, but only once the CCPA's own
applicability thresholds are met (business meets a revenue or data-volume test). This note does not
verify those thresholds against Dialecta's own numbers; that is `research/2026-az-no-comprehensive-
privacy-law.md` and adjacent notes' job, not this one's.
