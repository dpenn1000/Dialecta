# Copyright Alliance on the DMCA safe harbour (a rightsholder trade association)

**Source:** Copyright Alliance, "DMCA Safe Harbor", educational page, no author or date shown,
read 2026-09-21 through the fetch tool's extraction. **A rightsholder trade association**, so its
framing leans away from platforms; read as a cross-check on the Copyright Office, not as a guide.
https://copyrightalliance.org/education/copyright-law-explained/the-digital-millennium-copyright-act-dmca/dmca-safe-harbor/

## Summary

It lists four threshold requirements that apply to most service providers: "adopting and
implementing a policy of terminating the accounts or subscriptions of repeat infringers",
"informing subscribers and account holders of the repeat infringer policy", accommodating
standard technical measures, and "designating agents to receive takedown notices from copyright
owners and recording those designated agents with the U.S. Copyright Office". For hosting it adds
compliance with the takedown and counter-notice process and no prior awareness of the
infringement. It does not walk through the counter-notice or reinstatement process.

## Implies for Dialecta

- **It agrees with the Copyright Office on everything the drafts rely on**, and it adds one thing
  the Copyright Office page states less plainly: the repeat infringer policy has to be *told to
  users*. The Copyright section of the terms does that in one sentence, which is the cheapest
  version of the requirement.
- Its silence on counter-notices is characteristic of the source's interest, and the drafts take
  the counter-notice process from the Copyright Office instead.
