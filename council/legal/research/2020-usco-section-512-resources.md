# Section 512: what the hosting safe harbour asks for, and what notices must contain

**Source:** US Copyright Office, Section 512 resources and walkthrough, publication date shown as
May 21, 2020, read 2026-09-21 through the fetch tool's extraction. A regulator's plain-language
summary of 17 U.S.C. Section 512, not the statute text.
https://www.copyright.gov/512/

## Summary

For the 512(c) safe harbour, material stored at the direction of a user, a service provider must:

- designate "an agent to receive DMCA takedown notices and [register] this agent with the U.S.
  Copyright Office", and publish the agent's contact information on its website;
- "adopt and reasonably implement a policy to terminate repeat infringers";
- "accommodate and not interfere with standard technical measures";
- remove material on "actual knowledge" or "awareness of facts or circumstances from which
  infringing activity is apparent", without any "affirmative obligation to monitor";
- respond "expeditiously to remove or disable access to material claimed to be infringing upon
  receipt of a valid takedown notice";
- "not receive a financial benefit directly as a result of infringing activity, in a circumstance
  where you can control such activity."

A valid notice carries a signature, identification of the work, the location of the material,
the sender's contact information, a good-faith statement, and an accuracy statement under penalty
of perjury. A counter-notice carries a signature, identification of the removed material and its
prior location, a statement under penalty of perjury of mistake or misidentification, and contact
information with consent to federal jurisdiction. After a valid counter-notice the provider "may
repost the material in question not less than ten, nor more than fourteen, business days" unless
the rightsholder files suit.

## Implies for Dialecta

- **The Copyright section of `drafts/terms-of-service.md` is drafted to these elements.** Each
  numbered item there maps to one element here, so a lawyer can check it line by line.
- **Permanence yields to a valid notice, and the terms say so.** A hardened comment that infringes
  is removed on notice like anything else; the licence the terms take from members cannot license
  someone else's work.
- **The repeat infringer policy is a condition of the safe harbour, not a courtesy.** The terms
  state it, and the threshold is left to Dan as `[REPEAT INFRINGER RULE]`. A policy that is written
  and never applied is the failure this element exists to catch.
- **The financial benefit limb is the one to watch once memberships exist.** It needs both a direct
  benefit from infringement and the right and ability to control it, and a flat membership fee
  unrelated to infringing material is ordinarily not a direct benefit (unsourced; the lawyer review
  should confirm).
- Not legal advice. The statute text, 17 U.S.C. 512(c)(3) and (g)(3), was not read directly; the
  counter-notice jurisdiction wording in the draft follows the statute's known form and should be
  checked against it.
