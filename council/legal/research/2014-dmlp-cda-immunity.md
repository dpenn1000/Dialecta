# DMLP on CDA immunity: mixed content is the unlit part of the map

**Source:** Digital Media Law Project, "Immunity for Online Publishers Under the Communications
Decency Act", Berkman Klein Center for Internet and Society, Harvard University, read 2026-09-20.
Archived; the project stopped updating in 2014.
https://www.dmlp.org/legal-guide/immunity-online-publishers-under-communications-decency-act

## Summary

The page confirms that the interactive computer service definition is read broadly enough to
cover blogs, forums and listservs, so a site of Dialecta's size is inside it. On editorial
choices it restates the settled rule: "exercising traditional editorial functions over
user-submitted content, such as deciding whether to publish, remove, or edit material, is
immunized under Section 230", and an operator "will not lose immunity unless edits materially
alter the meaning of the content."

Its most useful sentence for this seat is an admission of uncertainty. On content created jointly
by the operator and a user, it says "Relatively few court decisions ... have analyzed the scope
of this immunity in the context of 'mixed content' that is created jointly by the operator of the
interactive service and a third party." It also restates the boundary that no amount of 230
argument crosses: "Section 230 does not immunize the actual creator of content."

## Implies for Dialecta

- Dialecta being small does not put it outside 230, and it does not put it outside a lawsuit
  either. The definition is about function, not scale.
- The mixed content gap is where Dialecta's comment card actually sits, and this source says the
  case law is thin there. That is a reason for the standing position on the tier label to carry
  low confidence rather than to pick a winner. A thin body of law means the answer depends on
  which court and which facts, which is a lawyer's question.
- The Stage 2 self-declaration flow is the clearest mixed-content surface on the platform: the
  contributor picks a tier from a set Dialecta defined, and Dialecta publishes the pair. That is
  content created jointly in the sense this page describes, and it is the surface most likely to
  be argued either way.
- Not legal advice, and eleven years stale. Filed for the mechanism and for the gap it names.
