# Blair v. Rent-A-Center: California's public-injunction rule survives the Federal Arbitration Act

**Source:** Blair v. Rent-A-Center, Inc., 928 F.3d 819 (9th Cir. 2019), read 2026-09-21 **at second
hand**, through search-result summaries of the opinion and of Ballard Spahr and National Law Review
alerts. The slip opinion was located and not read.
https://cdn.ca9.uscourts.gov/datastore/opinions/2019/06/28/17-17221.pdf

## Summary

The Ninth Circuit held that the Federal Arbitration Act does not preempt the rule of McGill v.
Citibank, N.A. (Cal. 2017): a contract term waiving a consumer's right to seek public injunctive
relief in any forum, court or arbitration, is unenforceable under California law.

## Implies for Dialecta

- **An arbitration clause written for a national membership would need a California carve-out or a
  severance clause from the first day,** because a Californian's claim under the Unfair Competition
  Law can seek a public injunction and no clause can take that away. That is part of the drafting
  cost of arbitration counted in `drafts/README.md`.
- It matters only if Dialecta adds arbitration. The drafts do not.
